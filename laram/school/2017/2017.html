LARAM School 2017

 The next Edition of LARAM School will be held

@ University of Salerno

4 - 15 September 2017
.
Applications are now CLOSED, deadline: 6 June

http://www.laram.unisa.it/school/2017/application_phd_students

http://www.laram.unisa.it/school/2017/registration_young_doctors

 

  • 40 selected PhD students must pay their own travel to Salerno, accommodation and a reduced registration fee of 250 euro (Applications are now OPEN, deadline: 6 June)
  • 5 selected PhD students sponsored by "XII IAEG Congress, Torino 2014" (http://www.iaeg.info/), providing a scholarship of 1000 euro, to applicants enrolled in a PhD program in or native of emerging Countries (Afganistan, Angola, Bangladesh, Benin, Bhutan, Burkina Faso, Burundi, Cambogia, Central African Republic, Chad, Comoros, Congo Democratic Republic, Djibouti, Equatorial Guinea, Eritrea, Ethiopia, Gambia, Guinea, Guinea Bissau, Haiti, Kenya, Kiribati, Korea Dem. Rep., Lao People's Democratic Republic, Lesotho, Liberia, Madagascar, Malawi, Mali, Mauritania, Mozambique, Myanmar, Nepal, Niger, Rwanda, Sao Tome & Principe, Senegal, Sierra Leone, Solomon Islands, Somalia, South Sudan, Sudan, Tajikistan, Tanzania, Timor-Leste, Togo, Tuvalu, Uganda, Vanuatu, Yemen, Zambia, Zimbabwe). (Applications are now OPEN, deadline: 6 June).
  • 5 registered Young Doctors must pay their own travel to Salerno and a registration fee of 400 euro (Registrations are now OPEN, deadline 22 June)

 

SCHOOL PROGRAMME

The Programme of the School, will be similar to those of Previous Editions since 2006, including:

S1 - SESSION "Landslide risk theory"

S2 - SESSION "The role of advanced methods in landslide risk analysis"

S3 - SESSION "Landslide case studies"

S4 - SESSION "Landslide risk analysis and zoning"

S5 - SESSION "Landslide monitoring and mitigation"

S6 - SESSION "Landslide risk management and risk governance".

 

Accommodation for all the students:

A special fare is available for the students of the LARAM School, for about 30 euro /night, at Ave Gratia Plena Youth Hostel (www.ostellodisalerno.it), located in the very centre of Salerno city, including:

  • Accommodation in double room,
  • Breakfast,
  • Direct private shuttle bus to/from University Campus

For direct reservation, it is enough for the applicant to choose this option in the application form.

 

Selection of LARAM 2017 students

The list of the selected students will be issued by 22 June 2017.

 

More details for IAEG scholarships:

Each of the 5 scholarships offered by "XII IAEG Congress, Torino 2014" will cover: i) the reduced registration fee to the School, 250 euro; ii) accommodation and breakfast in Salerno, transfer to/from University Campus, lunch at University Canteen, 400 euro; iii) a 350 euro reimbursement for the trip from/to your Institution venue to/from University of Salerno to be spent at a travel agency, which will be communicated to selected students.
