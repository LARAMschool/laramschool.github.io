.
The LARAM international training course in Hong Kong (China) is an initiative jointly conceived by the University of Salerno (UNISA), the University of Hong Kong (HKU), Geotechnical Engineering Office (GEO) of the HKSAR Government, and the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. The aim the initiative is to develop and conduct a training course dealing with landslide risk management, to be delivered by renowned landslide experts of international reputation.
.

DATES

  • June 2-7, 2025
  • from 8:00 a.m. to 6 p.m. Chinese Time
  • IN PERSON

.

REGISTRATION FEE and DEADLINE

  • "Early bird registration for the LARAM Course 2025 is now available at a discounted rate until the deadline of 25th March 2025"
  • "REGISTRATION DEADLINE: Early Bird (25th March); Standard (10th April)" 
  • If you are interested, please send an email to cechoi@hku.hk with your CV
  • see details below

.
VENUE

  • The LARAM course 2025 in Hong Kong will be at the University of Hong Kong (i.e., 17th ranked university QS global rankings).

.

PROGRAMME

  • The Course programme will consist of 8-hour daily lectures and tutorials from Monday to Friday. On Saturday 7 June, a field trip will be organized to visit the Po Shan Drainage Tunnel and Yiu Hing Road Landslide. More information will be available soon. 

.

PARTICIPANTS

The LARAM class will be composed by 40-50 selected participants:

  • Students (PhD; MPhil: MSc) (40 participants)
  • Young Professionals including postdoctoral fellows, research associates, engineers, research assistants, early career academics (10 participants)
  • Applicants should be less than 40 years of age before the start of the course

.

SELECTION

  • The selection of the participants will be based on the well-establish selection procedure of the LARAM School
  • The selected participants will pay their own travel and accommodation expenses, as well as a registration fee

.

ACCOMMODATION

  • Accommodation at the University of Hong Kong for PhD students and Postdocs will be available for HK$354 / night for single private room
  • Each room is air conditioned. Bathrooms will be shared. Each person is provided a bed, pillow, bedsheet, blanket, chair and, wardrobe. Towels not provided.
  • A detailed program and registration information will be provided shortly.
