1° LARAM Workshop 2006

Landslide risk management in European and extra-European countries

(Gestione del rischio da frana: esperienze e prospettive in paesi europei ed extra-europei)

 

6 - 7 September

Sala del Teatro, Villa Rufolo

Ravello (SA)

 

Traduzione simultanea Inglese-Italiano 
Simoultaneous translation Italian/English/Italian is provided. 

____________________________________________________________________________


TOPICS

Landslide risk management in Campania region and Italy
Tendencies in landslide risk management policies in European Countries
Landslide risk management in overseas Countries
The scientific aspects of landslide risk assessment in Italy
Landslide risk assessment and mitigation: experiences in Brazil
Perspectives for landslide risk zoning
Landscape protection as a necessary cultural issue
Italian urban planning for natural disasters
Idrogeological Planning: Landslide Risk
The activities of the International Centre for Geohazards for landslide risk management
Perspectives for hazard assessment of rapid landslides
