2° LARAM Workshop 2007

 Landslides risk: theory, zoning and mitigation
(Teoria, zonazione e mitigazione del rischio da frana) 
 

5 - 6 September

Villa Rufolo

Ravello (SA)


The official language of the Workshop is Italian.
Simoultaneous translation Italian/English/Italian is provided.

 

____________________________________________________________________________

 

TOPICS

Evaluating landslide risk: methods and experiences
Analysis of landslide susceptibility
Analysis of landslide hazard and risk
Experiences in the territories of the Amalfitana Coast and of the Basin Authority of the Liri-Garigliano e Volturno rivers

Landslide risk zoning at different scales
Experiences in Italy and in the Campania Region
Italian perspectives for zoning areas at high landslide risk
International Guidelines

Landslides risk mitigation and management in Italy, Europe and extra-European Countries
Emergency plans
Control works and warning systems
Land-use planning
