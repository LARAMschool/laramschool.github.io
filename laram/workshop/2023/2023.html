LARAM School 2023

17.00

End-of-school Certificate Ceremony for the LARAM Students

 

The International School on "LAndslide Risk Assessment and Mitigation" - LARAM, founded by the Geotechnical Engineering Group (GEG) of the University of Salerno in 2005, is envisioned to offer a permanent venue for PhD students, young researchers and renowned experts to interact and exchange ideas in the field of landslide risk.
